# Feature Specification: Focused Crop Rotation UX & Dynamic Multi-Field Solving

> **Specification Status:** Implemented & Verified  
> **Target Scope:** Crop Rotation Tab UX Decluttering (`StrategyComparison.tsx`, `TimelineGantt.tsx`), Single-Strategy Focused View with Switcher, Field-Specific Solving Engine Invariants  
> **Design Framework:** ChaiUI (chaicode.com look)  
> **Theme Policy:** **White Default (Soothing Warm Light Canvas `#fafafa`)**  
> **Author:** Antigravity / Engineering  
> **Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & User Directives

### 1.1. User Directive
> *"currently the crop rotation section is crowded ..... after selecting one strategy.....no need to show all other strategies....but the option of editing it is necessary....moreover no hardcoded data and different fields should be done properly"*

### 1.2. Root Problem Statement
1. **Screen Crowding & Cognitive Overload:**
   Displaying four full-sized strategy comparison cards simultaneously with high-density metrics on top of the seasonal timeline creates cognitive overload for a farmer on mobile. Once a strategy is active, the farmer primarily wants to see that plan's detailed schedule, immediate next steps, and benefits—with a clean, unobtrusive way to change or edit it.
2. **Field-Specific Engine Verification:**
   When switching between different fields (e.g., Field 1: 5-acre tubewell plot growing Rice-Wheat vs Field 2: 3-acre canal plot growing Cotton-Wheat), the rotation solver must dynamically adapt the crop sequences, water deficits, and fertilizer savings strictly to each field's specific:
   - Coordinates & Live NASA precipitation / temperature / root-zone wetness
   - Soil texture (Clay vs Loam vs Sandy Loam)
   - Irrigation access (Groundwater borewell vs Canal surface vs Rainfed vs Drip)
   - Baseline crop history (Standing crop & previous crop)

## 2. Focused UX Architecture

### 2.1. Focused Active Strategy View (No Crowding)
- When on the Crop Rotation tab:
  - Display the **Active Strategy Card** in high polish with ChaiUI hairlines and typography:
    - Title & Archetype tag
    - Impact Strip: Urea bags saved, Tubewell water conserved, Soil health status, Pest shield
    - Two clear action buttons:
      1. **"Change plan"** (allows switching to another curated strategy via an elegant popover or modal drawer)
      2. **"Customize crops"** (opens the interactive rotation sandbox to tweak individual crop slots)
- Only when the farmer taps "Change plan" or "Compare all 4 plans" does the comparison view expand or show the other choices.

### 2.2. Seamless Field Switcher on Crop Rotation Page
- Prominent top bar showing:
  - Active parcel name (e.g., "Main Parcel - 5 acres")
  - Location and exact NASA POWER grid coordinates
  - Standing crop and days into season
  - Instant parcel dropdown to switch to other saved fields without leaving the page.
- Switching parcels synchronously updates:
  - Latitude, longitude, and NASA climatology
  - Field soil texture & organic matter
  - Irrigation regime
  - Curated strategies generated by `generateCuratedStrategies(region, climate, soil, priorities, activeField)`

---

## 3. Dynamic Solving Logic Across Different Fields
- `generateCuratedStrategies` must verify:
  - If field irrigation is `rainfed`: automatically prioritize drought-hardy pulses/millets and penalize high-water crops like puddle rice.
  - If field soil is `sandy_loam`: prioritize deep-rooting legumes and increase water percolation loss factor.
  - If field soil is `clay`: prioritize aeration taproots to alleviate compaction.
  - If field current crop is `cotton` or `wheat` or `corn`: start subsequent rotation with complementary botanical families to ensure zero pathogen overlap.

## 4. ChaiUI Design Rules
- Background `#fafafa`, cards `#ffffff`, borders `border-stone-200/80`.
- One highlight phrase per section:
  `font-montserrat font-medium tracking-tight capitalize text-yellow-800 dark:text-orange-100`.
- Asymmetric corner buttons (`btn-asym`, `btn-asym-mirror`).
- Zero emojis (Lucide icons only).
- Sentence case, no em dashes.
